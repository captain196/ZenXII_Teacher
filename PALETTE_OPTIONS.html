ZenXii Teacher · Theme proposal

Minimalist & Soothing Color Palettes

Three research-backed directions for the Teacher app. Each is built for long grading & attendance sessions: muted, desaturated accents, generous neutral surfaces, and one calm brand color.

✕ Gold concept removed — no more #D4AF37

🧠 Calm = focus

Soft blue & sage green lower arousal — slower heart rate, less tension — by triggering the parasympathetic "rest" response. Best-documented focus colors.

CogniFit · Villa Healing Center

🎚️ Muted > saturated

Saturated color raises visual arousal & cognitive load. Desaturated palettes cut competing signals, so the eye rests and focus lasts longer.

Adobe Express · Shift eLearning

◻️ Restraint = minimalist

Few tones, lots of warm/cool neutral, one accent. Removes distraction and pushes attention to content — a refined, timeless feel.

Figma · MockFlow

Option A — Recommended

Sage & Sand

The most research-backed for focus + grounding. Muted sage accent on warm off-white — spa-like, organic, zero glare. Best choice for hours of grading and attendance.
